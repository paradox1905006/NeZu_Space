NeZu Space is a multi-tab browser where every tab behaves like its own browser. Each tab keeps its own cookies, storage, cache and history, so logins and sessions never mix.

<b>Profiles</b>
Group your tabs into profiles, each with its own colour, picture, search engine, home shortcuts and downloads. Swipe the bottom bar to change profile.

<b>Tabs and gestures</b>
Swipe the address bar to change tab, swipe a tab card or profile row to close it. Pin tabs, give them a name, colour or picture.

<b>Home screen</b>
A native home with shortcut tiles that show the website's picture. Add your own picture, sort, edit, share, or pin a shortcut to your phone's home screen.

<b>Downloads</b>
Per-profile downloads with pause and resume, add a download by pasting a link, open, copy link, download again or delete the file.

<b>Looks</b>
Material 3 and Material You colours, light and dark theme, and your own background picture, animated GIF or looping video with adjustable opacity and an optional video sound. Book page-turn and dust animations when you change, close or create tabs and profiles, which can be switched off.

<b>Sounds</b>
Optional sound effects for tab and profile change, delete, create and app start. Every sound has its own switch and preview, and you choose your own audio (only the app open sound has a default).

<b>Privacy and security</b>
App lock with fingerprint, face or phone lock, locked and private profiles, data cleared when the app closes, tracking removed from links, tracker and third-party cookie blocking, HTTPS only, Safe Browsing, per-site camera, microphone and location choices, warnings for risky downloads and certificate errors, and tabs and profiles stored encrypted.

<b>More</b>
Built-in ad blocker, long-press menus for images and links, cookie tools, per-profile Clear data (cache, form data, history, web storage, cookies), and a "Don't sleep" option that keeps the screen on.

NeZu Space has no account, no analytics and no tracking. Isolation uses the WebView multi-profile API and needs a recent Android System WebView (110 or newer).
