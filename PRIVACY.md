# Privacy

NeZu Space does not collect, store on a server or send any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising SDK.

- Browsing data (cookies, storage, cache, the history list of Menu > History), tabs, profiles, shortcuts and settings stay on your device, in the app's private storage.
- Pictures, GIFs, videos and sounds you choose are copied into the app's private storage and never uploaded.
- The app talks to the internet only to load the pages you open, to read a website's icon when you add a shortcut, and to download files you ask for. Websites you visit can of course see your requests, as in any browser.
- Permissions: Internet, notifications (download progress), media images (choosing pictures) and, on Android 9 and older, storage (saving downloads). Camera, microphone and location are declared but only requested when a website you allowed asks for them; choices are saved per site and can be reset in Settings > Privacy & Security > Site permissions.
- Settings > Privacy & Security adds an app lock, private profiles, clearing of data when the app closes, tracker and cookie blocking, HTTPS only and encrypted storage of tabs, profiles and the download list (key in the Android Keystore). Pictures, shortcut icons and downloaded files are stored normally.
