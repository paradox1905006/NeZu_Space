# Privacy

NeZu Space does not collect, store on a server or send any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising SDK.

- Browsing data (cookies, storage, cache, history), tabs, profiles, shortcuts and settings stay on your device, in the app's private storage.
- Pictures and sounds you choose are copied into the app's private storage and never uploaded.
- The app talks to the internet only to load the pages you open, to read a website's icon when you add a shortcut, and to download files you ask for. Websites you visit can of course see your requests, as in any browser.
- Permissions: Internet, notifications (download progress), media images (choosing pictures) and, on Android 9 and older, storage (saving downloads).
